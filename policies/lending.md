# Lending SOP (RBI digital lending aligned)

1. Only pitch if the eligibility model says eligible and score ≥ `min_score`.
2. Never pitch within 24h of an unresolved complaint.
3. Daily EMI must not exceed `max_emi_to_gmv_pct`% of average daily GMV.
4. Share the Key Fact Statement and capture **explicit consent** before any application.
5. Loan submission is always approved by a human.
6. If the merchant declines, do not re-pitch for `cooldown_days_after_decline` days.
