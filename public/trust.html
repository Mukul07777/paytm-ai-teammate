Our principles

Six promises we never break.

🔒

People decide the big things

Lending and large money movements always need a human. No setting can change that.

🧪

Rehearse before acting

Every money or hardware action is simulated first, with safety checks you can see.

✍️

Consent comes first

Clear terms and explicit consent before any credit product. A “no” is always respected.

🚫

Never pay twice

Duplicate-payment checks run before every retry. Actions are never repeated automatically.

📜

Rules in plain language

Policies are written in simple words your operations team owns — and Dost follows them exactly.

🧾

A complete record

Every thought, action and approval is logged and can be exported for review.

Before Dost gets more freedom, it has to pass the test. Every behaviour — paying out, lending, escalating, learning — is checked against our policies in a safe, separate world, every time we change anything.
