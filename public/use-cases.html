Use cases From first alert to final “thank you”

Real problems. Finished, not just answered.

Here's how Dost handles the moments that matter most to a merchant — and to the people who support them.

Merchant service

Fixed before anyone calls.

Device outage

The Soundbox goes quiet

A shopkeeper can't hear payments arrive, and sales start slipping.

  1. Dost notices the device stopped checking in and sales dropped.
  2. Tries a remote recovery — the cheapest fix first.
  3. Calls the merchant in Hindi and switches payment alerts to their phone.
  4. Rehearses the dispatch, then sends a field agent with a new device.
Payout failure

The settlement bounces

Money the merchant needs for suppliers is stuck at the bank.

  1. Traces the payout and finds why it failed.
  2. Rules out any chance of paying twice.
  3. Retries safely — or asks a human when the amount is large.
  4. Confirms on WhatsApp with the bank reference.
Upset customer

“You charged me twice!”

A customer is angry, and getting angrier.

  1. Confirms the double debit and starts the reversal right away.
  2. Speaks with empathy — and hears that they want a person.
  3. Hands the case to a human with a three-line brief.
  4. The human starts ahead. Nobody repeats themselves.
Compliance

A re-KYC deadline approaches

Miss it, and a merchant's payouts freeze.

  1. Finds every merchant due in the coming days.
  2. Sends a personal reminder in each one's language.
  3. Shares a one-tap video-KYC link.
  4. Follows up gently with anyone who hasn't finished.
Merchant growth

Growth, offered responsibly.

Working capital

A busy season is coming — and stock is running low

Dost sees a merchant's sales climbing and helps them get ready, without pushing.

  1. Checks eligibility and makes sure repayments stay comfortable.
  2. Calls with a clear, honest offer — in their language.
  3. Shares the full terms and waits for explicit consent.
  4. A human approves every loan. If the merchant says no, Dost respects it.
